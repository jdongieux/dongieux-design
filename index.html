The Studio

Design that
feels like you.

Dongieux Design brings a rare mix of creative boldness and California ease to every project. Based in Santa Barbara, the studio is known for spaces that are layered, joyful, and deeply personal — never predictable, always livable.

The work draws from the textures of the California landscape — bleached oak, raw linen, fired clay, sea mist — translated into rooms that feel curated, soulful, and effortlessly alive.

How we work
together.

01
Discovery

A deep conversation about how you live — your rituals, your references, your non-negotiables. We listen more than we talk. This is where your vision takes shape and we start to understand what makes you, you.

02
Concept

Spatial planning, mood, material palette, and key pieces — presented as a singular, cohesive vision for your review. We push until it feels exactly right. Nothing gets approved until you love it.

03
Curation

We source every element with intention — trade vendors, vintage fairs, artisan makers across California and beyond. Nothing generic. Everything chosen. Your home will never look like a showroom.

04
Installation

White-glove delivery and installation, styled to the final detail — the last pillow, the last book, the last stem in the vase. You walk in and it's home — exactly as imagined, better than expected.
